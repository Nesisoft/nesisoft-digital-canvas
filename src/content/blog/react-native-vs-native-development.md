Choosing how to build a mobile app is one of the earliest and most consequential decisions a team makes. The choice between cross-platform and native development shapes cost, timeline, and the experience you can deliver.

## The Case for React Native

A single codebase targeting both iOS and Android can dramatically reduce time and cost. For content-driven apps, internal tools, and many startups, that efficiency is decisive — and the gap with native has narrowed considerably.

## When Native Wins

- **Performance-critical apps:** Games, heavy animation, and intensive media processing.
- **Deep platform integration:** Apps that lean on the newest OS capabilities.
- **Long-term, large teams:** Where platform-specific polish justifies the duplicated effort.

## A Practical Framework

Ask three questions: How performance-sensitive is the app? How much does it depend on platform-specific features? What are your team's existing skills? The honest answers usually point clearly to one approach.

## Conclusion

There is no universally correct answer — only the right answer for your product, team, and timeline. Match the tool to the job rather than the trend.
