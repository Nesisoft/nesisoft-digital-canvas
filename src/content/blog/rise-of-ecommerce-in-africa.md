E-commerce in Africa is experiencing remarkable growth, driven by rising smartphone penetration, a young population, and increasingly creative approaches to payment and delivery.

## What's Fueling the Growth

A mobile-first internet, the spread of mobile money, and a generation comfortable buying online are combining to expand the market rapidly. Local platforms are tailoring the experience to conditions that global players often overlook.

## The Real Challenges

- **Logistics:** Reliable last-mile delivery remains the hardest problem in many regions.
- **Trust:** Cash-on-delivery still dominates where buyers are wary of paying upfront.
- **Payments:** Fragmented payment rails require flexible, multi-method checkout.

## Building for Local Conditions

Winning platforms treat installment payments, offline-friendly experiences, and mobile money as first-class features rather than afterthoughts. Meeting customers where they are is what turns interest into orders.

## Conclusion

The opportunity is enormous for businesses that design around Africa's realities instead of importing assumptions. The next decade of African commerce will be built by those who solve trust, logistics, and payments together.
